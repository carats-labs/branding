List 2: Design Tokens and Their Locations

color-stroke-all: Used for all thick structural outlines surrounding the diamond shapes and the "TS" monogram.

color-fill-center: Used for the primary fill of the large "TS" letters.

Background & Core Depth

color-facet-1: Core Background — The inner facets located directly behind and between the "TS" letters. Determines the base mid-tone depth of the diamond's interior.

Left Side Facets

color-facet-15: Upper-Mid Left Face — The large, prominent facet on the upper left side of the diamond.

color-facet-3: Far Left Top Rim — The outermost triangular facet on the top left edge.

color-facet-4: Inner Left Top Rim — The triangular facet on the top edge, situated between the far left and the center.

color-facet-5: Top Left Inner Highlight — The internal facet just below the top left rim. Typically a brighter shade to simulate top-down lighting.

color-facet-6: Topmost Rim (Left) — The left half of the absolute top flat edge of the diamond.

Center & Right Side Facets

color-facet-11: Top Center Face — The central downward-pointing triangle directly below the top flat rim. Usually a strong highlight.

color-facet-2: Upper-Mid Right Face — The large, prominent facet on the upper right side of the diamond.

color-facet-7: Inner Right Top Rim — The triangular facet on the top rim, between the far right and the center.

color-facet-8: Top Right Inner Face — The internal facet just below the top right rim.

color-facet-9: Far Right Top Rim — The outermost triangular facet on the top right edge.

color-facet-10: Topmost Rim (Right) — The right half of the absolute top flat edge of the diamond.

color-facet-14: Top Right Accent — A very small, supplementary highlight facet positioned along the top right edge.

Lower Shadows & Accents

color-facet-12: Lower-Mid Outer Shadows — The steep outer facets on both the left and right lower-middle sections. Typically requires a dark, rich shade to give the bottom half 3D depth and weight.

color-facet-13: Bottom-most Accents — The lowest thin slivers on the left and right that converge at the bottom tip. Originally purple, these are perfect for the darkest shadow or a striking, vibrant contrast color.
